Here we see one of the trademarks of Phenomenology and Continental philosophy in general. Subsequent conceptual analyses are posited as being part of the experience of something, or, indeed, even part of that thing. So, a thing's being white becomes also a thing's being 'the-opposite-of-black', or 'pure-negative-directed-at-blackness' or some such nonsense. But this 'negativity' is only something the phenomenologist has subsequently thought of. It's not even part of the experience of a white object, and it's certainly not a part of whiteness itself.
